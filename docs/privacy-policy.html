Privacy Policy

Overlearn

Last updated September 3, 2026

The short version

  • Overlearn collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no crash reports, no network requests of any kind.
  • Everything you enter — segment names, practice results, history — stays only on your device, in local app storage.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, because none was ever made.

What Overlearn is

Overlearn is a practice tool for musicians. You create named practice segments, run timed correct/incorrect drills against them, and review your history for each one. It works entirely offline and has no server component — there is nothing for it to talk to.

What data we collect

None. Specifically, Overlearn does not use, and has never included in its codebase:

  • Any networking library — the app cannot make an internet connection even if it wanted to
  • Analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • Crash or error reporting services
  • Advertising identifiers or ad networks
  • Accounts, sign-in, or any form of user identification

This isn't a policy promise layered on top of the app — it's a property of what's actually built. You can verify it yourself; the source is public.

What's stored, and where

The segments you create, your practice session state, and your completed-session history are stored locally on your device only, using on-device storage private to the app. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service by the app itself (Overlearn explicitly disables Android's automatic app-data backup for this reason)
  • Is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app

Your device or operating system may offer its own backup features outside Overlearn's control. Overlearn does not enable or participate in any of them.

Permissions

Overlearn requests no device permissions — no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notifications. The app has no use for any of them.

Children's privacy

Because Overlearn collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. The app is suitable for any age of user for that same reason.

Changes to this policy

If Overlearn's data practices ever change, this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above, before any such change ships in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the app's listing on Google Play.

Overlearn is an independent, offline-only practice app. This page describes its actual behavior, not aspirational policy language.
